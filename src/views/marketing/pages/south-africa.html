What is Fitnexis?

Fitnexis is gym management software for independent gyms and fitness businesses. It connects day-to-day administration with membership billing, class bookings and member self-service. It runs in a supported web browser on desktop or mobile; a native app is not required.

How do cash, EFT and online payments work?

Cash and EFT records help your team keep invoices and balances up to date. Recording an EFT does not automatically verify a bank transfer: your team should check it against the payment received. Online checkout uses Paystack after the gym completes the required payment setup. Provider fees, eligibility and configuration are separate from Fitnexis subscription pricing.

How do you get your gym started?

  1. Request a walkthrough and explain how your team currently manages members and billing.
  2. Register your gym through the gym registration page. Registrations are reviewed before activation.
  3. The authorised owner verifies their email and sets up the gym workspace.
  4. Configure membership plans, staff access, classes and payment settings for the capabilities you intend to use.
  5. Give members their gym-specific sign-in address. Existing members can use Find your gym.

Can existing member records be imported?

Discuss your current records during onboarding. A self-service spreadsheet import tool is planned and is not currently available. Keep a copy of your source records and agree the onboarding approach before moving data.

What should you check when choosing gym software?

  • Can your front desk find a member, check them in and see their membership information?
  • Can your team distinguish recorded payments from money actually received?
  • Do class capacity, booking and payment rules fit your timetable?
  • Can staff access the tools appropriate to their responsibilities?
  • Are current capabilities clearly separated from roadmap features?
  • Are subscription prices, payment-provider fees and onboarding requirements clear?
